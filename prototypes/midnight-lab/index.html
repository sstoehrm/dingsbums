dingsbums

Open a session, share its link, and draw together in real time. The German word for a thing whose name you can't think of right now. A whiteboard is full of them.

SessionKept 15 min after the last tab closes

Frames, stickies, text, shapes Connections Undo · redo Export .tar 12 colour themes

Send a link. Draw together.

No accounts and no setup. Everyone on the link edits the same board live; the server keeps it in memory while someone has it open.

Objects

Frames, notes, shapes

Frames, sticky notes, text, circles, rectangles, stars and arrows, with fill and text colours, groups and locking.

Structure

Connections and layers

Connect any two objects. Bring a selection to the front or send it to the back, with Home and End as shortcuts.

Clipboard

Paste anything

Paste images and text onto the board. Copies keep their layout and connections, also into another board.

Canvas

Infinite, with a hand

Scroll to pan, Ctrl and the wheel to zoom. Hand mode turns a drag on empty canvas into a pan, for mice.

Archive

Export and import

Take a board home as a .tar file and bring it back into any session later.

Themes

Midnight, daylight and ten more

Pick a theme per browser or follow the OS light and dark setting.
